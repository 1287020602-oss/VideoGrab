VideoGrab bundles yt-dlp (The Unlicense) and an FFmpeg executable supplied by
imageio-ffmpeg. FFmpeg is licensed under the LGPL/GPL depending on its build.
It also bundles Node.js (MIT license) as the JavaScript runtime required by
some supported sites.
Public workspace connectivity uses cloudflared (Apache License 2.0).
The desktop interface uses CustomTkinter (MIT license) and Pillow (HPND).

yt-dlp: https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp
FFmpeg: https://ffmpeg.org/
imageio-ffmpeg: https://github.com/imageio/imageio-ffmpeg
Node.js: https://nodejs.org/
cloudflared: https://github.com/cloudflare/cloudflared
CustomTkinter: https://github.com/TomSchimansky/CustomTkinter
