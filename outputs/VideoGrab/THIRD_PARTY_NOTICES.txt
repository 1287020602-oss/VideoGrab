VideoGrab bundles yt-dlp (The Unlicense).
The shipped FFmpeg executable identifies itself as FFmpeg 7.1 essentials_build
from www.gyan.dev, configured with --enable-gpl and --enable-version3.
Its own -L output states GNU GPL version 3 or any later version.
FFmpeg is a separate executable; VideoGrab does not modify its binary.
Build provider: https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/
Upstream version source: https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/tree/n7.1
License: https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/blob/n7.1/COPYING.GPLv3
FFmpeg licensing and external-library details: https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html
It also bundles Node.js (MIT license) as the JavaScript runtime required by
some supported sites.
Public workspace connectivity uses cloudflared (Apache License 2.0).
The desktop interface uses CustomTkinter (MIT license) and Pillow (HPND).
